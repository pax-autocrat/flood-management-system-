FLOOD SAFETY DOS & DON'TS

MCG GURUGRAM & NDMA PROTOCOL ADVISORY
WHAT TO DO (DO'S)
  • Turn off main circuit breaker and LPG cylinder valve before floodwaters enter premises.
  • Move to upper floors or elevated community shelters with pets and vital identity documents.
  • Use elevated 6-lane arteries like Golf Course Road and SPR bypass; completely avoid low underpasses.
  • Keep 72-hour survival kit: potable water, dry rations, torch, power bank, and ORS packets.
  • Monitor 1800-180-1817 IVR or zero-data NeerNerta SMS alerts for rescue evacuation updates.
WHAT NOT TO DO (DON'TS)
  • NEVER drive or walk through moving water above 15 cm (6 inches) — sweep-away risk.
  • NEVER touch fallen electricity poles, submerged junction boxes, or sagging wire lines (Dial 1912).
  • NEVER park vehicles in basement ramps or underground garages during Red Alert rainfall.
  • NEVER allow children or pets near storm drains, Khandsa canal embankments, or open manholes.
  • NEVER spread unverified rumors on WhatsApp; follow only verified MCG/GMDA ICCC advisories.
